They speak in circuits, code, and steel,
Where dreams are drawn and problems real.
Late nights lit by monitor's glow,
Debugging errors, row by row.

A bridge, a chip, a spinning gear —
Each solved by minds that engineer.
Failure's just a step, not end,
Each bug a lesson, not a bend.

From blueprint's line to product's birth,
They shape the tools that move the earth.
Unseen, unsung, yet everywhere —
The quiet hands that build and care.

So here's to those who calculate,
Who build the future, small and great —
Engineers, with patient hearts,
Turn simple thoughts to working arts.
